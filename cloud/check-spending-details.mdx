## Check current spending

To check the details of your spending during the current billing period:

1. Go to [**Billing**](https://cloud.risingwave.com/dashboard/billing).
2. Check the **Current Cycle** summary for the current billing period.
3. Use the **Cluster Spend** section to review usage and charges by cluster.
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud/check-spending-details/billing-current-spending.png"/>
</Frame>

## Check previous spending

To check the details of your spending during previous billing periods:

1. Go to [**Billing**](https://cloud.risingwave.com/dashboard/billing).
2. Scroll to the **Invoices** section.
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud/check-spending-details/billing-page-invoices.png"/>
</Frame>

3. Click on the invoice ID to view the detailed breakdown of the invoice.
