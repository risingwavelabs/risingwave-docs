---
title: "Manage payment methods"
---

You can manage your payment methods for your organization in RisingWave Cloud. The saved payment methods will be used to make automatic payments of the monthly bill after each billing period. You can also use the saved payment methods to settle outstanding payments manually if the automatic payment fails. All members of the organization can view and manage payment methods."

<Note>
Currently, RisingWave Cloud only supports credit cards as the payment method.

</Note>

## Add a payment method

To add a new payment method, follow these steps:

1. Go to [**Billing**](https://cloud.risingwave.com/dashboard/billing).
2. In the **Payment Methods** section, click **Add** or **Add payment method**.
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud/manage-payment-methods/billing-add-payment.png"/>
</Frame>

3. Fill in all necessary information on your card.
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud/manage-payment-methods/billing-add-payment-details.png"/>
</Frame>

4. Click **Confirm** to save your card.

## Delete a payment method

To delete an existing payment method, follow these steps:

1. Go to [**Billing**](https://cloud.risingwave.com/dashboard/billing).
2. In the **Payment Methods** section, find the payment method you want to delete.
3. Click the trashcan/delete button on the top right of the payment method you want to delete.
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cloud/manage-payment-methods/billing-delete-payment.png"/>
</Frame>
4. Click **Confirm** to confirm the deletion.
